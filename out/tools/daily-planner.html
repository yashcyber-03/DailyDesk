About this tool

The Daily Planner lets you plan each day with optional times, tick items off as you go and move between days. Plans are saved only in this browser.

How to use it

  1. Pick a day, add a plan with an optional time and press Add.
  2. Tick items when they are done. Use the arrows to move between days.
  3. Your plans stay in this browser and are not synced to another device.

Frequently asked questions

Can I plan future days?

Yes. Use the next-day arrow or pick any date.

How many plans can I save?

Up to 1,000 entries can be stored in this browser.
