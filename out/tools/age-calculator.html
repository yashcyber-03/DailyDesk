About this tool

The age calculator works out your exact age in years, months and days from your date of birth, and tells you how many days you have lived and how long until your next birthday. Change the 'Age at' date to find the age on any past or future day, for example for a form or an eligibility check.

How to use it

  1. Pick your date of birth.
  2. Leave 'Age at' empty to use today, or choose another date.
  3. Read your age in years, months and days, plus total days lived and the next birthday.

Frequently asked questions

How is the age counted?

Complete years and months are counted first, and the days left over after the last full month are shown as days.

What if I was born on 29 February?

In years that are not leap years, the next birthday is counted on 28 February.
