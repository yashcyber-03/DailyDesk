About this tool

BMI (body mass index) compares weight to height. This calculator accepts metric or imperial units, shows the WHO adult category and the weight range that corresponds to a BMI of 18.5 to 24.9 for your height.

How to use it

  1. Choose metric or imperial units.
  2. Enter your height and weight.
  3. Read your BMI, category and the healthy weight range.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. It does not separate muscle from fat and is meant for adults. It is a screening number, not a diagnosis.

Can I use it for children?

No. Children and teenagers are assessed with age-specific growth charts. Ask a doctor.
