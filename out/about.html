About DailyDesk

DailyDesk is an independent online toolkit. Instead of searching for a different website every time you need to merge a PDF, shrink a photo, work out an EMI or organize your day, you can find 27 tools in 4 categories in one clean place.

It is made for students, professionals, freelancers, business owners and anyone who wants quick, dependable help with everyday digital tasks.

What we care about

  • Useful first

    Each tool does one job well, explains how it works and tells you its limits.

  • Private by design

    Tools run in your browser. Files and productivity data are not uploaded to DailyDesk.

  • Honest

    Calculator results are aids, not professional advice. We explain limits and do not invent reviews or claims.

  • Works without sign-in

    Every available tool is accessible without an account. There is no account or sync service.

What is inside

  • PDF Tools: Merge, split, compress, rotate and convert PDFs right in your browser.
  • Image Tools: Compress, resize, convert and crop images, plus QR codes and color palettes.
  • Calculators: EMI, GST, BMI, age, percentage, discount and unit conversions.
  • Productivity: To-dos, notes, Pomodoro timer, planner, habit tracker and passwords.
